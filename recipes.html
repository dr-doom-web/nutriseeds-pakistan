GOOD TO KNOW

Preparation notes

Can chia seeds be swallowed dry?

Do not swallow spoonfuls of dry chia seeds. They absorb liquid and swell. For the illustrated recipe, fully soak them in liquid before eating. People with swallowing difficulties should seek professional advice.

How should flax seeds be used?

Ground flax is generally easier to digest than whole flax. Use food-safe seeds and store ground flax appropriately according to the supplier's instructions.

Are all pumpkin and sunflower seeds ready to eat?

Not necessarily. Confirm whether the specific product is shelled and ready-to-eat or intended for additional preparation. Follow the actual packet label.

Are these recipes medical advice?

No. These are general food ideas and do not replace dietary advice from a qualified professional.
