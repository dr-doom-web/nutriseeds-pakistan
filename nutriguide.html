OUR FUTURE VISION

Thoughtful guidance, without the pressure.

When a qualified dietitian partner is available, NutriGuide could offer optional dietary discussions about food choices and how seeds may fit into a person's existing diet.

Advice should be independent and evidence-based. A professional must be free to recommend no NutriSeeds products or suggest medical referral when appropriate.

We have not hired a clinician, do not offer consultations, and do not collect health histories on this site.
HOW IT COULD WORK

Support in three steps.

01

Request guidance

Register on the free waitlist now. This does not book an appointment; we will contact you if the service launches.

02

Talk with a professional

A qualified dietitian would review the customer's needs independently and privately.

03

Make an informed choice

Customers could decide whether any product fits their needs, without an obligation to buy.
