## 5. Technical Optimization Summary

All photographs have been downloaded and processed locally:
- **Formats:** Dual format delivery with high-efficiency `.webp` and universally compatible progressive `.jpg`.
- **Dimensions:**
  - Product Cards & Recipe Cards: Standardized 800 × 600 px (4:3 aspect ratio) with centered subject framing to prevent distortion or stretching.
  - Promotional Banner: 1600 × 700 px responsive high-resolution landscape asset.
- **Performance:** Compression tuned (JPEG Q85, WebP Q82) keeping typical file sizes between 23 KB and 150 KB for instant first-contentful paint.
- **Loading Behavior:** `fetchpriority="high"` on the above-the-fold homepage banner; `loading="lazy"` on below-the-fold catalog cards and recipes.
