## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

NutriSeeds Pakistan is an educational multimedia prototype designed to make edible seeds and seed-based nutrition accessible and easy to explore for Pakistani university students, working professionals, and everyday households.

This document records the online retail and wholesale price benchmarks gathered on **November 6, 2026** across major Lahore wholesale platforms (notably Akbari Mandi merchants) and direct-to-consumer e-commerce stores in Pakistan. The resulting demonstration prices balance affordability for entry-level consumers with realistic market benchmarks for sealed consumer packaging.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Academic Demonstration Disclaimer:**  
> All prices displayed on the NutriSeeds Pakistan website are researched demonstration estimates for academic evaluation and prototype testing. They are **not** approved commercial retail prices, live commodity exchange rates, or verified contractual quotes from suppliers. Commercial launch will require sealed supplier supply contracts, certified packaging unit economics, delivery zone tariffs, and formal Punjab Food Authority (PFA) licensing.

## 4. Value Proposition Alignment

NutriSeeds Pakistan has updated its core brand messaging away from positioning miniature 25g trial bags as its main business model. The verified value proposition states:

> *"We help Pakistani students, working people, and families explore edible seeds and seed-based foods by offering convenient product packs, a Discovery Box, simple usage guidance, and easy online ordering, so they can make informed choices without confusion."*

### Key Operational Strengths:
1. **Product Variety:** 5 core single seeds, 1 balanced blend, 1 beginner discovery sampler, and 1 planned cooking oil.
2. **Standardized Pack Sizes:** Predictable 200g, 500g, and 1kg tiers suited to individual trial, monthly pantry use, or family meal preparation.
3. **The Discovery Box:** Eliminates purchase hesitation by allowing customers to test four distinct seed types (100g total) for just Rs. 299.
4. **Accessible Culinary Guidance:** Jargon-free prep instructions and safe culinary pairing notes (soaking rules, grinding tips, shelf storage).
5. **Interactive Seed Finder:** A quick lifestyle quiz mapping kitchen routines (breakfasts, snacks, baking, salads) to the right seed.
6. **Dual Order Request Channels:** Frictionless submission via Formspree email confirmation or direct WhatsApp chat integration.
7. **NutriGuide Concept:** A vision for future free, non-commercial dietary guidance with an accredited nutritionist (waitlist currently collecting interest).

## 5. Costing & Packaging Considerations To Be Confirmed

Prior to transitioning this academic prototype into an operational commercial enterprise, the following financial factors require physical supplier verification:

1. **Food-Grade Packaging:**
   - 200g / 500g / 1kg three-layer barrier pouches with resealable zip locks and transparent oval product windows.
   - Food-contact safe Kraft cardboard packaging for the Discovery Box outer shell.
2. **Cleaning & Sifting Losses:**
   - Raw wholesale mandi seed lots typically exhibit a 3% to 7% sorting yield loss for dust, seed husks, and machine sorting.
3. **Delivery Charges:**
   - Standard domestic courier rates within Lahore (Rs. 150–200 per parcel up to 1kg) and nationwide across Pakistan (Rs. 250–350) remain strictly separate from product prices and are marked **"To be confirmed"**.
4. **Regulatory Licensing:**
   - Registration with the Punjab Food Authority (PFA), batch testing for moisture content, and nutritional facts validation.
